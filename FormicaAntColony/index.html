How the colony works. Workers leave the nest, explore the surface, and lay a food trail when carrying food home — other foragers follow it. Food is stored in storage chambers; nurses carry eggs to the nursery and feed larvae, which pupate and hatch into new workers or soldiers. Diggers expand the nest and pile soil into ant hills on the surface.

Chambers. Paint storage, nursery and queen zones with the Chambers tool. Ants route to them automatically through connected tunnels. Use Dig → Ant excavation to mark tunnels for the ants to dig themselves.

Danger. Spiders hunt ants, beetles steal food, rain drives foragers underground, and cold slows everything. Rival colonies grow, and raid when they have enough soldiers.
